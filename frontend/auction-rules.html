Auction Rules

Everything you need to know before the bidding begins.

THE BASICS

How the Auction Works

01

Join the Room

Enter an auction room using the room code provided by the host.

02

Get Your Budget

Every team starts the auction with a fixed budget to spend on players.

03

Player Goes Up

A player is introduced with their role, country and base price.

04

Start Bidding

Teams compete by placing bids while staying within their available budget.

05

Player Sold

The highest valid bid wins the player and the player's price is deducted from that team's budget.

PLAY FAIR

Auction Rules

Squad Rules

Build your squad by successfully bidding for players during the auction.

Bidding Rules

Each new bid must be higher than the current highest bid.

Budget Rules

A team cannot place a bid that exceeds the amount of money available to them.

Player Rules

Players can only be purchased once during an auction.

REMEMBER

Spend wisely. Bid smart.

Once your budget is spent, you won't be able to use it again. Plan your squad before making big bids.
